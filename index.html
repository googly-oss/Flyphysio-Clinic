Helpful information

Before your visit.

Have a question? Call or message the clinic for details specific to your needs.

How do I book?

Send an appointment enquiry through the form or contact the clinic by phone or WhatsApp. The clinic will confirm availability.

What should I bring?

Bring relevant medical reports, prescriptions, scan results, and comfortable clothing if movement assessment is expected.

Do I need a referral?

Referral requirements can vary. Contact the clinic to confirm what applies to your situation.

Are all treatments suitable for everyone?

No. A physiotherapist should assess your needs and discuss the appropriate care options and any precautions.

What are the clinic hours?

The supplied listing says open, closes at 2 PM and reopens at 3 PM. Please call to confirm today's schedule and closing time.
